College game day outfits

Game day looks that go all day.

From tailgate mornings to the final whistle, build a fun, comfortable outfit you can move in. Keep school colors front and center without losing your personal style.

Style guide

Simple ideas you can actually wear

Use these outfit formulas as inspiration rather than shopping lists. Swap in colors, items, and accessories you already love.

These are fictional editorial examples for a class project; no products are offered for sale.

Start with school-color basics

Pick one team-color top, jacket, or accessory, then pair it with neutral jeans or a skirt. A small pop of color keeps the look wearable after the game.

Make comfort part of the outfit

Choose shoes made for walking and standing. Add a crossbody bag that meets venue policies, and bring a light layer when evenings cool off.

Repeat your favorite pieces

Wear the same jeans and sneakers with a different top or hair accessory next Saturday. Personal touches make a repeat outfit feel new.

Keep exploring

Style changes with the plan. Try college game day outfits, college going out outfits, affordable college outfits, or seasonal outfit ideas.
